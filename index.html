About me

From measurements
to understanding.

Cambridge, United Kingdom

I’m a Research Associate at the University of Cambridge’s Cavendish Laboratory, working on computational imaging and scientific inference.

I develop physics-based algorithms to recover spatial and wavelength-dependent structure from sparse optical-interferometric observations. My work combines forward modelling, Bayesian inference, uncertainty quantification, and scientific machine learning.

Previously, I worked on electromagnetic inverse problems, sensor selection, and field prediction. I hold an M.S. and Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from IIT Madras.

Selected research & software

Physics meets computation.

01

Bayesian optical imaging

Jointly reconstructing spatial morphology and spectral evolution from sparse, noisy interferometric measurements. I build Python/JAX/NIFTy workflows for forward modelling, variational inference, posterior sampling, and fit diagnostics.

Current research · Python / JAX / NIFTy
02

Measure less. Recover more.

Sensor and frequency selection for subsurface radar imaging, using physical operators and subspace methods to identify informative measurements. The public MATLAB implementation includes a demo and numerical validation scripts.

Explore the code ↗
03

Learning to design antennas

Co-developed a tandem neural-network approach to multiband antenna inverse design, combining an inverse network with a pretrained CNN surrogate of electromagnetic simulations.

Published research · PyTorch / CNNs / Inverse design
04

Agents for scientific reconstruction

I am developing an LLM-assisted workflow that assesses reconstructed images alongside posterior uncertainty, measurement resolution, fit diagnostics, and model parameters to support subsequent experiments.

Work in progress · Scientific workflow automation
